# BRIEF — COVERED marketing site

**Engagement** (one-of-a-kind-designer, 2026-07-30): make coveredaccess.com more creative
within two hard constraints set by the client: the 4-colour palette is locked
(violet #472549 · orange #F0820B · lavender #B49CDB · grass #B9A00F) and the logo
is a fixed asset (own colours, no redesign).

- Medium: `web` · Register: `expressive` · Seed: `42` · chaosBudget: `2`
- Operators (seed-derived, documented because the engine binary was unavailable at
  selection time): `scamper`, `lateral-thinking`

## Users
1. People who menstruate, caught short in a public place — urgent, bodily, minutes matter.
2. Venues (pubs, unis, gyms) deciding whether to host a unit — want zero cost/hassle, full credit.
3. Brands deciding whether to buy ad slots — want dwell time, gratitude, proof.

## First principles (Deconstruct → Classify → Reconstruct)
Fundamental truths (irreducible): a period can start unexpectedly; stigma silences
asking for help; in this model someone else has already paid, so the user owes
nothing at the moment of need.

- HARD: palette (client decree), logo (client decree), licensed brand fonts
  (Motor / Rumble Brave / Open Sauce), WCAG AA, Eleventy + Decap CMS bindings
  (titles/copy are editor-owned text — design may not depend on rewriting them).
- SOFT: section order, card shapes, motion style, decorative motifs.
- ASSUMPTION (rejected): "more colour = more creative." Colour saturation was
  already done; creativity here must come from *behaviour and meaning*.

## Strategy sentence (design-from-able)
**"Matter-of-fact. Period."** — COVERED destigmatizes by stating things plainly and
ending the sentence. The copy already speaks in short declaratives ("Brands pay.
You don't."). The new logo already ends in an orange full stop. The site's job is
to make that full stop the brand's own mark — the pun (full stop = period) does
the destigmatizing work silently, and the transaction proof (your total: £0.00)
does the model's work tangibly.
